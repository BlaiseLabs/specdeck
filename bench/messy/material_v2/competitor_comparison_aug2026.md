**Stockroom Stationery Co.** (Manchester, bigger than us, ~25 staff)
- Pro membership: £49/yr, 10% off, 30-day invoice terms. They push it hard on every page.
- Their bulk is order-value based (£500+), 12% off the whole basket — but you get EITHER Pro OR bulk, not both. Clever for them.
- They quoted Northgate 12% on everything over £500 + free delivery over £30. (Gareth told us.) That's their standard offer, nothing bespoke.
- Delivery is taxed. Their "inc tax" prices hide this a bit.
- Gift card bonus in December: buy £50 get £55. Odile thinks there's something odd about how they account for this, not our problem.

**Paper & Pine** (Bristol, similar size to us, very Instagram)
- Two tiers of bulk: 10% at 50+ units of a product, 20% at 200+. (Dan: this is the one Theo's old supplier copied, I think.)
- Bulk applies to their "Last Chance" sale stuff too. Seems mad to me, they must be losing money on it.
- Pine Points: free, 1pt per £1. Basically 5% back but only as future credit.
- Evergreen: £30/yr, 15% off on top of bulk (they add them, so 200+ units as Evergreen = 35% off?!), and free delivery always. Jules has seen this and wants it. See her 2027 doc.
- Free delivery over £60 — measured on the basket BEFORE discounts. So a £65 basket that's £50 after discount still gets free delivery. Generous.
- They show prices ex-tax.

**Us**
- We're cheapest or near-cheapest on list price for most of the café and school stuff.
- Where we lose: no codes, no price match, no paid tier, higher flat delivery.
- Where we win: we're local to the schools, Mark answers the phone, our bulk + Friends combo isn't bad on 100+ lines.
