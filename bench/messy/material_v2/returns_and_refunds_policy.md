# Returns & Refunds

*fernhillsupply.com/returns — page text as published, last updated 2 September 2026*

---

We want you to love what you buy from Fernhill. If something isn't right, here's how to send it back.

## The short version

- You can return most unused items within **30 days** of delivery.
- Fill in our returns form, pack the items up, and post them back to us.
- Once we've received them, we'll refund you within **5 working days**, to the payment method you used.
- **Clearance** items can only be returned if they're faulty.
- **Gift cards** can't be returned or refunded once bought.

## What can I return?

Anything you've bought from us that is:

- unused, and in a condition we can sell again;
- in its original packaging where it had any (shrink-wrapped packs should still be sealed);
- returned within 30 days of the day it was delivered to you.

You don't need to give us a reason, though it really helps us if you do.

### Things we can't take back

- **Clearance items**, unless they're faulty. Clearance products are end-of-line, discontinued patterns or packaging seconds, and we sell them at or near cost, so we're not able to take them back for a change of mind.
- **Gift cards.** Once a gift card has been bought it can't be returned or exchanged for cash. If a gift card has been lost, get in touch and we'll do our best to help.
- **Opened packs of cups, lids and sleeves**, for hygiene reasons. Unopened sleeves of cups are fine.
- Items that have been written in, stamped, cut or otherwise used.

## How do I return something?

1. Fill in the returns form at fernhillsupply.com/returns/form. You'll need your order number (it starts with ORD-).
2. We'll email you a returns reference within one working day.
3. Pack the items securely and write the returns reference on the outside of the parcel.
4. Post it to: Fernhill Supply Returns, Unit 4, Brookfield Trading Estate. (The full address is on your returns email.)
5. Keep your proof of postage until your refund comes through.

Return postage is paid by you, unless the item is faulty or we sent you the wrong thing — in which case tell us before you post it, and we'll send you a prepaid label.

## How much will I get back?

We refund the amount you paid for the items you return.

If your order had a discount on it — for example our bulk discount or your Fernhill Friends member discount — your refund is based on the discounted price you actually paid for the returned items, not the full list price.

If you're returning only some of the items on a line (for example 30 out of 120), we'll refund the price you paid for those 30. We won't re-price the rest of your order.

**Delivery charges** aren't refunded for change-of-mind returns. If the item is faulty, damaged, or we sent the wrong thing, we'll refund the delivery charge too (if you paid one).

## How long does it take?

- We aim to check returned parcels within 2 working days of them arriving.
- Refunds are issued within 5 working days of that check.
- Your bank or card provider may take a further 3–5 working days to show the money in your account.

If you paid with a Fernhill gift card, the refund goes back onto a gift card.

## Faulty or damaged items

If something arrives damaged or develops a fault, please email support@fernhillsupply.com with your order number and a photo. We'll send a replacement or refund you in full, including any delivery charge — whichever you prefer. You don't usually need to send a damaged item back; we'll tell you if we need it.

You have the same rights for faulty clearance items as for anything else.

## Exchanges

We don't do direct exchanges — it's quicker to return the item for a refund and place a new order for the one you want. If you'd like us to hold stock of the replacement while your return is in the post, just ask.

## Larger and business orders

If you're a school, café or business account returning a large quantity, please contact us before sending anything back so we can arrange collection. For orders paid on invoice terms, we'll issue a credit note instead of a card refund.

## Questions?

Email support@fernhillsupply.com — we answer Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm, usually within one working day.
