### [PRICE] Bulk discount — how it works

Hi {name},

Great question! Our bulk discount is 15% off any product where you're ordering 100 or more of that item on a single line. So 100 A4 pads would get the discount on the pads; 60 pads and 40 notebooks wouldn't, because neither line reaches 100.

{If relevant:} Items in our Clearance section aren't included, as they're already at their lowest price.

Best,
{me}

> *Sam's note: don't say "100 items in your order". It's per line. If someone's at 99, the line isn't bulk.*

### [PRICE] Fernhill Friends — what do members get?

Hi {name},

Thanks for joining Fernhill Friends! 🌿 As a member you get an extra 5% off. If any of your lines also qualify for our bulk discount, the 5% is taken off after the bulk discount.

The member discount doesn't apply to Clearance items or to gift cards.

Best,
{me}

> *Sam's note: if they ask "so is that 20% on bulk?" — no. Say "15% bulk, then 5% off the reduced price". Don't give them a combined %.*
