# Q4 2026 campaign brief — "A Fernhill Winter"

Owner: Jules K. · v2, 30 Sep 2026 (rewritten after the September review) · For: Priya, Dan, Sam, Mark · Agency copied on sections 3 and 5 only

---

## 1. What we're doing

Six weeks of steady, low-cost marketing from 2 November to 18 December, aimed at:

1. **Gift buyers** — notebooks, pencil cases, desk organisers and gift cards.
2. **Small businesses and cafés** stocking up for the busy season.
3. **Schools** ordering for the spring term before the Christmas break.

No new discounts, no codes, no sale. Priya at the review: "We're not a sale shop. We sell nice things at fair prices." This brief is built around that.

## 3. Messages

### Core message
*Thoughtful, recycled stationery from a small local shop. Real people, fair prices.*

### Supporting messages (approved)
- "Join Fernhill Friends — it's free, and members save a little extra."
- "Stocking up? Order 100 or more of any eligible item on a line and save 15% on it."
- "Free UK delivery on orders of £50 or more (after discounts)."
- "Gift cards from £10 — the present that's never the wrong size."

## 7. What we'll measure

- Orders and revenue vs Q4 2025 (last year we were at £21,400 for Nov–Dec, target £27,000).
- Fernhill Friends sign-ups (currently 461; target 650 by end of December).
- Average order value (currently £42.30).
- Share of orders in the £50–£60 band — not because we're changing anything, but because I want the number for the 2027 conversation.
- Support emails tagged [PRICE] — Sam to keep a weekly count. If they spike, we look at the wording.
