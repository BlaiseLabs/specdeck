## 1. Free shipping threshold

### Today
Flat £5.99 delivery, free when the basket is £50 or more. That's measured on the basket before discounts.

> **Mark (comment, 3 Sep):** after discounts, Jules. it's what's left once the bulk/Friends discounts come off. the whole cups-guy saga was about exactly this. please fix in the doc so dan doesn't read it and quote it
>
> > **Jules (reply):** argh, yes, sorry — after discounts. Leaving my mistake visible so this comment makes sense. Everything below assumes after-discount.

### Proposal
**Raise the free-shipping threshold to £75 from 1 February 2027** (after the January sale, so we're not changing it mid-campaign). Keep the £5.99 flat fee as is.

### Why
- Our average order value is £41.80. A big chunk of orders sit in the £50–£60 band (19% of orders) — people are adding a little extra to clear £50, which is the threshold doing its job. My guess is a £75 threshold would drag a decent share of the £55–£70 orders up to £75.
- The agency deck (benchmark of 40 small UK homeware/stationery shops) has a median free-shipping threshold of £60 and a quarter of them at £75+.
- Shipping cost us an average of £4.10 per parcel in August. On orders between £50 and £75 we're eating all of it.

### Risks (Dan's and Sam's, not mine, but fair)
- Schools and cafés place lots of mid-sized orders. Dan thinks they'd feel this the most.
- Sam says "why didn't I get free shipping" is already the #2 support question. A higher threshold = more of those.
- Customers might split purchases across retailers.

### What I'd want to test first
A/B on the site for 4 weeks in January: half of visitors see £75, half see £50. Measure conversion and order value.

## 2. Fernhill Friends — a second tier

### Today
Friends is free. Members get an extra 5% off, after any bulk discount. Applies to everything.

> **Sam (comment, 4 Sep):** not everything — it doesn't go on clearance, and gift cards don't get it either (nothing goes on gift cards). we've had a few customer emails from people who read "everything" in the launch newsletter. could you not write "everything" in the next one please 🙏
>
> > **Jules (reply):** you're right, sorry. The launch newsletter said "on your orders", not "everything", but I take the point. Not clearance, not gift cards.

### Proposal
Add a paid tier, working name **Friends+**:
- £24 a year.
- **20% off** for Friends+ members (instead of the 5%), still after any bulk discount.
- Free shipping on every order for Friends+ members, no threshold.
- Early access to clearance (24 hours before everyone else). Not a discount on clearance — just first pick.

The free tier stays exactly as it is (5%).

### Why
- 20% is the number Theo and half the café accounts keep asking for. If they want it, they can pay for it.
- £24 × even 60 members = £1,440 a year up front, plus it locks them in.
- The paid-tier model is what both of our main competitors have moved towards (see Dan's competitor sheet, though neither of them is doing exactly this).

### Numbers (very rough)
| | Free Friends today | Friends+ (proposed) |
|---|---|---|
| Members | 412 | 40–80 (guess) |
| Discount | 5% | 20% (proposed) |
| Fee | £0 | £24/yr (proposed) |
| Avg spend/member/yr | ~£180 | ~£600 (café/school types) |
| Discount cost per member | ~£9 | ~£120 |
| Fee income per member | £0 | £24 |

So on these numbers Friends+ costs us roughly £96 a member a year net unless it makes them spend more. I know. I *know*. That's why this is a draft.

## 3. Bigger bulk tier

### Today
15% off a line with 100 or more of the same item. Doesn't apply to clearance or gift cards.

### Proposal (Dan's idea, written up by me)
Two tiers:
- 100–249 on a line: 15% (as now)
- **250+ on a line: 20%**

### Why
- Our three biggest café accounts order 300–500 cups a month. A higher tier would make us harder to leave.
- Northgate's central orders are 300+ for several lines.

### Against
- Margin on cups at 20% would be about 9%. That doesn't pay for Kofi's time packing them.
- Mark points out the Greenleaf minimum order is already squeezing storage.

> **Priya (comment, 11 Sep):** No for 2027. Maybe look again if Greenleaf drop their price.
